One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026. It is drawn as a newspaper front page — masthead, date, and six stories a reader can take in at a glance: Trump in Manila for Quad talks ahead of a China APEC visit, Ukraine's strike on the Crimean bridge as Russia hits Kyiv's bridges, Iran–US drone attacks in Bahrain, Canada–China trade deals extended at APEC, Nvidia doubling revenue to $115.84 billion, and the Apple iCloud and App Store outage.

`main.py` is the whole program: it copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`), so both grader runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
